# Appendix A - Timeline

Dated public milestones, each with a source. Fiscal years end January 31 (fiscal 2026 =
February 2025 to January 2026).

| Date | Milestone | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Before 2024 | FieldRoutes (pest, lawn care) and Aspire (commercial landscaping) acquired and offered alongside ServiceTitan | [1] |
| Feb 8, 2024 | FCC confirms AI-generated voices are "artificial" under the TCPA (FCC 24-17) | [2] |
| Apr 2024 | Convex Labs acquisition (commercial sales and marketing platform); month per secondary source | [3][11] |
| May 1, 2024 | Utah Artificial Intelligence Policy Act takes effect | [4] |
| Dec 11, 2024 | IPO priced at $71 per share (8.8M shares) | [5] |
| Dec 12, 2024 | Trading begins on Nasdaq as TTAN; closes first day at $101 | [5][6] |
| Jan 31, 2025 | Fiscal 2025 ends: GTV $68.5B, ~9,500 Active Customers | [3][7] |
| May 7, 2025 | Utah SB 226 amendments to AI disclosure rules take effect | [4] |
| Fiscal 2026 | Atlas introduced as an agentic AI layer; Conduit Tech (LiDAR HVAC design) added | [3] |
| Jan 31, 2026 | Fiscal 2026 ends: GTV $82.1B, ~10,800 Active Customers, revenue ~$961M | [3][8] |
| Mar 12, 2026 | Fiscal 2026 results; "Agentic Operating System" framing; plan to double Max capacity | [8][9] |
| May 7, 2026 | OpenAI announces gpt-realtime-2 (configurable reasoning effort, spoken preambles, parallel tool calls) | [12] |
| Jul 6, 2026 | OpenAI releases gpt-realtime-2.1 and gpt-realtime-2.1-mini | [13] |

| Sep 10, 2026 | OpenAI brings GPT-Live-1 (full-duplex voice with delegation) to the API | [15] |
| Oct 6, 2026 | Pantheon 2026 keynotes (Orlando): Max expansion, 30 agents with a coordination layer, Homh, Atlas mobile app, Adaptive Capacity for AI CSRs | [16][17] |
