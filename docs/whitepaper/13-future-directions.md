# 13 - Future Directions

**Estimated reading time:** 5 minutes

## Five Takeaways

1. Atlas and AI Virtual Agent are public product directions, not a private roadmap. [1][2]
2. Public pages support assistance, retrieval, booking, and workflow automation claims.
3. Outbound, multimodal, predictive-capacity, and technician-copilot extensions are
   hypotheses, not commitments.
4. Experiments should test value without locking in architecture.
5. Timing, investment, sequencing, and success criteria remain `[unverified]`.

## Public Direction

Atlas is publicly described as an assistant that can answer from ServiceTitan data and
support field work, while office features include “coming soon” language. [1] AI Virtual
Agent is publicly positioned around booking, confirmation, rescheduling, adaptive
capacity, and escalation. [2] These statements define public direction only.

## Hypotheses

**Hypothesis:** outbound reminders or confirmations could reduce avoidable contact-center
work if consent, timing, language, and escalation are correct.

**Hypothesis:** multimodal technician assistance could reduce retrieval time if answers are
grounded in current equipment information and the user can inspect the source.

**Hypothesis:** predictive capacity could improve booking quality if its objective and
uncertainty are visible and policy always overrides an attractive prediction.

Each hypothesis needs a narrow pilot, a baseline, harm metrics, and a stop condition.
