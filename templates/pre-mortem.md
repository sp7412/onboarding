# 90-Day Pre-mortem: run with your manager in week 2

Ask your manager: "Imagine it's day 90 and my first three months went badly. What most likely
happened?" Write down every answer without debating it, then turn the top risks into goals,
check-in questions or early warning signs in your 30/60/90 agreement. Takes 15 minutes;
surfaces real worries faster than "what does success look like?"

| Possible failure | How likely (low/med/high) | Early warning sign | What we'll do about it |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | |

## Prompts if the conversation stalls
- Picked the wrong first problem, or one nobody needed.
- Moved too slowly for the team's pace, or too fast without context.
- Didn't build trust with a key partner (PM, evals owner, infra, support).
- Shipped something that hurt a metric or a customer.
- Spent too long learning without contributing, or contributed without learning.
- Expectations drifted and nobody said so.

## Fictional example

| Possible failure | How likely | Early warning sign | What we'll do about it |
|---|---|---|---|
| First project was a local optimum the team didn't need | med | No one asks about it in standup | Validate the problem with the PM in week 3 |
| Too much polishing before sharing | high | Docs arrive late and complete | Share rough drafts within two days |
