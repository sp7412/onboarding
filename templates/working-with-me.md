# Working With Me: one page to share in week 1

Fill this in before your first 1:1 and share it with your manager (and later, close
teammates). It answers the questions a good manager would ask anyway, so the first 1:1 can
spend its time on the team. Keep the real version in company systems, not this repo.

- **How I like to communicate:** written first or talk first; Slack vs. doc vs. call;
  how quickly I usually respond.
- **When I do my best focused work:** hours, and how I protect them.
- **Working hours and time zone:** including any fixed commitments.
- **How I like feedback:** direct and early; written or in person; what helps it land.
- **How you'll know I'm stuck or frustrated:** and what helps.
- **What I'm strong at and happy to offer:** e.g. evaluation design, out-of-distribution
  detection, edge and latency work, rigorous testing.
- **What I'm new to and learning:** e.g. SaaS release pace, the trades, production voice agents.
- **What I need from you in the first 30 days:** access, introductions, context, a first problem.
- **Recognition:** what kind feels meaningful to me (public, private, in writing).

## Fictional example

- **How I like to communicate:** written first; I'll post a short update before asking for a
  meeting. Slack for quick questions, docs for anything I want feedback on.
- **How I like feedback:** direct and early, even if it's rough. "One thing to do more of, one
  to do less of" every couple of weeks works well for me.
- **What I'm new to and learning:** product release cadence. Tell me if I'm polishing too long.
