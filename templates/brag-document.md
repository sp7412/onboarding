# Brag Document: running record of work and impact

Start on day 1; add to it every Friday (5 minutes). Link each entry to a goal in your
30/60/90 agreement. Share it with your manager before each check-in and review. Based on
Julia Evans, "Get your work recognized: write a brag document"
(https://jvns.ca/blog/brag-documents/). Keep the real version in company systems.

## Goals this period
- (copy from the 30/60/90 agreement)

## Projects and outcomes
- **What:** … · **Impact:** what changed and for whom, with a number if there is one · **Goal:** #

## Work that's easy to miss
- Reviews, mentoring, incidents helped, docs written, questions answered, glue work.

## Things I learned
- …

## Feedback received
- Who, when, what they said (short paraphrase).
