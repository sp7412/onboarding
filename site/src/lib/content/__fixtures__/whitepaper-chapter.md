# 07 - AI Voice Agents

**Estimated reading time:** 7 minutes · **Facts as of:** September 27, 2026

## Five Takeaways

1. Voice agents are a front end to scheduling logic, not free-form chat.
2. Escalation paths are part of the product, not an afterthought.
3. Quality loops span recordings, outcomes, and customer-visible metrics.
4. Deployment modes shape the telephony layer.
5. Guardrails live in the application, not the prompt.

## What the product does

The product handles overflow and after-hours calls with capacity-aware booking.
